New Orleans po-boys

7 po-boy shops for New Orleans' sandwich on crackly French bread: roast beef debris, fried shrimp, oysters or catfish, “dressed” with lettuce, tomato, pickles and mayo. Each was checked in fall 2026 against a 2025–26 source.

Ask for early accessThe free Bayou Eats app for iPhone and iPad is on its way to the App Store.

Dressed or plain

A po-boy is built on New Orleans French bread, light inside with a crust that shatters. Order it “dressed” for lettuce, tomato, pickles and mayonnaise. Two places here are MICHELIN Bib Gourmands (Parkway Bakery & Tavern and Domilise's), and the backstreet shops of Jefferson Parish keep their own following. Many close early or on Sundays, so check the hours.

The list at a glance

Towns with the most: New Orleans (6), Metairie (1).

Our method: a place made the list only if a 2025 or 2026 source backed it up (its own site or menu, a dated local news story or an official tourism listing) and its street address matched. No current source, or a website gone dark, and it stayed off. Review sites played no part, and places run by region and town rather than by anybody's score. Po-boy shops and boudin counters keep short hours and sell out, and crawfish is seasonal, so check before you drive.
