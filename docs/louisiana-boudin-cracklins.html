Louisiana boudin & cracklins

8 stops in Acadiana for boudin, the Cajun sausage of pork and rice, sold hot by the link or fried into balls, and cracklins, fried pork skin. Each was checked in fall 2026 against a 2025–26 source.

By the link, by the pound

Boudin is pork, rice, onions and spices in a casing, steamed and sold hot from meat markets and gas-station counters across Acadiana; eat it out of the casing in the parking lot. Every stop here is on the Cajun Boudin Trail kept by Lafayette's visitors bureau. Scott calls itself the boudin capital of the world. Most sell out by the afternoon.

The list at a glance

Towns with the most: Lafayette (3), Scott (1), Jennings (1), Carencro (1), Breaux Bridge (1), Youngsville (1).

Our method: a place made the list only if a 2025 or 2026 source backed it up (its own site or menu, a dated local news story or an official tourism listing) and its street address matched. No current source, or a website gone dark, and it stayed off. Review sites played no part, and places run by region and town rather than by anybody's score. Po-boy shops and boudin counters keep short hours and sell out, and crawfish is seasonal, so check before you drive.
