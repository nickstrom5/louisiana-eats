What counts as an honor here

Two sources, both reported as facts rather than scores. One is the MICHELIN Guide American South, whose first selection came in November 2025 and whose 2026 selection was revealed on October 21, 2026, with Emeril's at two stars. The other is the James Beard Foundation: its winners and America's Classics awards from any year (4 places). Where an honor went to a chef, the line under the place says so. Reader polls, "best of" roundups and review-site stars don't count.
