Terms of use

The plain-language terms for the Bayou Eats app and this website. Last updated 2026-11-03.

What the app is

Bayou Eats is a free guide to restaurants in Louisiana. It is provided as is, for personal use, without charge and without warranties of any kind.

Check before you go

Restaurants open, close, change their hours and change their menus, and many po-boy shops and boudin counters keep short hours. Dishes and founding years are what each place or a named source said when we checked in fall 2026. We work to keep the lists right, but we can't promise that any listing is current or complete. Call the restaurant before you make the trip.

Inspections and public records

The app and this site link each place to the Louisiana Department of Health's inspection search; they show no inspection results and compute no grade. License numbers and types come from the City of New Orleans' Occupational Business Licenses and Alcohol Beverage Outlets and from East Baton Rouge Parish's business registrations and ABC licenses, as of the dates shown. For official records, use each agency's own site.
